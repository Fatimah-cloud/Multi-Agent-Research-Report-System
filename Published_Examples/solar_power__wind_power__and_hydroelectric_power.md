Renewable energy sources play a critical role in the global transition toward sustainable electricity generation. Solar power utilizes photovoltaic cells or concentrated sunlight to convert solar radiation directly into electric current. Wind power captures kinetic energy through large turbines, which are often organized into expansive wind farms to feed the electrical grid. Hydroelectric power complements these sources by harnessing the gravitational flow of water through dams to drive turbines and generate consistent, reliable energy. Together, these technologies provide a diverse and increasingly essential foundation for the world's power supply.
