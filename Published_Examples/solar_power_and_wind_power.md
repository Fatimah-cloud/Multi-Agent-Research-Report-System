Solar power generates electricity by converting sunlight into energy, primarily through photovoltaic panels or concentrated solar systems that drive steam turbines. Complementing this, wind power utilizes turbines to capture kinetic energy from air currents, typically organized into large-scale wind farms connected to the electrical grid. While solar technology relies on the photovoltaic effect to harness light, wind power has evolved from historical mechanical uses to become a major global energy source. By 2025, wind power alone contributed over 8% of the world's total electricity supply. Together, these renewable technologies represent critical pillars in the transition toward sustainable energy production.
